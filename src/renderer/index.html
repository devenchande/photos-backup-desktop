Photos Backup

Desktop backup to Google Photos

Back up your photos to Google Photos

Connect a Google account, choose the folders to back up, and the app uploads each photo and video in original quality (or Storage Saver). Items already in Google Photos are skipped automatically.

  1. Sign in with Google in a private window — the token never leaves this machine.
  2. Pick the folders that hold your library.
  3. Press Back up now. That's it.

Uses Google's private Photos endpoints; the integration may stop working without notice. Experimental software — use at your own risk.

    Backup

    What these options do
    • Storage Saver: Google Photos makes files smaller.
    • Count against storage quota: off, uploads don't use your Google storage.
      • The Google Photos app may still label them “Storage saver”; the file size shows the real quality.
    • Date from filename: dates a file from its name (IMG_20240709_182027.jpg) when it has no date inside.
    • Live Photos: an iPhone photo and its video (IMG_1234.HEIC + .MOV) upload as one Live Photo.
      • To copy them from an iPhone: in its Photos settings set Transfer to Mac or PC to Keep Originals, then import over USB.
      • Off, the video uploads as a separate video.
    • Allow re-upload: shows Re-upload Photos… and Re-upload a Folder….
      • Uploads files again, even ones Google already has, so they can move to the free quota.
      • Only helps with Count against storage quota off.
      • Google may keep its old copy: try a few files and compare your storage.

    Support

    • Credentials, addresses, filenames and URLs are left out.
